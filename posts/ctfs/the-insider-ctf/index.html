Question 2#

From looking at their GitHub account, what hacking tools did the person of interest use on company PCs for:
i) Lateral Movement & privilege escalation
ii) Remote Control
iii) Reconnaissance
iv) Cryptocurrency mining
v) Password cracking

Question 3#

Find our person of interest's other accounts online:
i) From a popular business/hiring network
ii) Social Media account for taking photos
iii) Community profile for playing PC games and what university club were they in?

Question 4#

Using their social media find:
i) Where did they go on holiday?
ii) Which city do they live in?
iii) Where is their family based?

Question 5#

You have been provided with a picture of the building in which the company has an office. Our person of interest works remotely. Which city is the company located?

Question 6#

With the intel you have provided, our ground surveillance unit is now overlooking the person of interest's suspected address
They saw them leaving their apartment and followed them to the airport. Their plane took off and has landed in another country
Our intelligence team spotted the target with this IP camera. Which state and country is this camera in?
